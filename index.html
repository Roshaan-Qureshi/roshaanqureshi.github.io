About Me

I am an Electrical Engineering student at COMSATS University Islamabad, with a strong interest in embedded systems, battery management systems, microcontrollers and electrical system design.

My academic and practical work focuses on developing electrical and embedded solutions, with particular interest in lithium-ion battery systems, active cell balancing, STM32 microcontrollers and MATLAB/Simulink.

I am currently working on my Final Year Project, focused on a capacitor-based active cell balancing system for a three-cell lithium-ion battery pack.
